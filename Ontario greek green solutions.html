Ontario Greek Green Solutions

About the Project

Ontario Green Solutions is an innovative environmental company based in Ontario, dedicated to promoting sustainable development and green technology. The Brand Guideline Project was created to establish a consistent and professional visual identity that reflects the company’s eco-conscious values and forward-thinking mission. It defines the use of logo, colors, typography, and tone of communication to ensure coherence across all internal and external materials — from stationery to digital media.

‍

Problem

Before the creation of the brand guideline, Ontario Green Solutions lacked visual consistency across its communications. Logos, colors, and typefaces were used inconsistently, leading to a fragmented brand image that didn’t fully represent the company’s innovative and sustainable vision. This inconsistency created confusion among partners and weakened brand recognition across digital and print platforms.

‍

Challenge

The main challenge was to design a cohesive and scalable brand identity that communicates sustainability, innovation, and professionalism. The visual system needed to remain flexible enough for various applications — from eco-campaigns to corporate materials — while maintaining a unified look and feel.

‍

Solution

The final brand guideline delivers a clear, modern, and eco-focused identity system. A minimalist design approach was adopted, combining natural green tones, balanced typography, and structured layouts to create a professional yet approachable look. All applications — including logo variations, stationery, apparel, and digital icons — follow strict visual standards to ensure brand integrity. The result is a strong and recognizable visual identity that communicates trust, innovation, and environmental responsibility.
